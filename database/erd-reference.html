MT AUDIT / AuditIQ Version 1 schema

Audit Schema Ledger

The complete Version 1 entity relationship design for the continuous audit platform — 34 tables across 10 modules, built to keep one chain intact: business process → risk → control → audit test → data source → execution → evidence → exception → finding → remediation → re-test.

10

UUID

01 Organization 02 Access 03 Processes 04 Risk & Control 05 Data Sources 06 Audit Testing 07 Monitoring 08 Evidence & Exceptions 09 Findings & Remediation 10 Governance
Two tables added beyond the original list. gateways now exists because data_connections.gateway_id had nothing to point to — it carries the registration-code lifecycle (single-use, time-limited, tenant-bound) and device-certificate fingerprint described for the Audit Data Gateway. risk_categories was designed alongside risks earlier but dropped from the final table count — restored here as risks.risk_category_id. No raw credentials are modeled anywhere: data_connections.secret_reference is a pointer into a vault, never a password.
